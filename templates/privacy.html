Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also collect the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — along with account activity like login timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating the summaries and category breakdowns you see in your dashboard. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it is permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and email delivery. These providers only receive the minimum data necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can reach us at support@spendly.app.
